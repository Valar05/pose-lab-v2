# Swarmling Style Anchor V1 Prompt

Mode: built-in image generation with three native Blender render references.

Create a polished production character concept-art sheet for the Fleshpunk
beat-'em-up enemy called the Swarmling.

## Reference roles

- Image 1 is immutable front-view anatomy and silhouette reference.
- Image 2 is immutable side-view anatomy and silhouette reference.
- Image 3 is immutable movement and pose evidence from the real rig.

Use all three to preserve the existing creature identity. Improve appearance
only; do not redesign its anatomy or reinterpret its motion.

## Subject invariants

Preserve the squat humanoid proportions, oversized smooth eyeless cranium, open
vertical feeding mouth, long arms, ragged/webbed hands, heavy thighs, wide
splayed feet, and the asymmetric root/tendon quills erupting from the shoulder
blades, spine, hips, thighs, and calves. The dorsal root organ must remain the
defining silhouette. No eyes, no horns, no tail, no armor, no weapon, no
clothes, no hair, no extra limbs, and no generic goblin or zombie features.

## Appearance pass

Anchor it in Fleshpunk: pale gray-lilac living flesh with controlled bruised
blue-green undertones; faint subdermal veins; old graft seams; damp cellar grime
on hands and feet; dorsal roots that read as living tendon, nerve, and vascular
tissue rather than decorative spikes; subtly raw pink root tips and mouth
tissue; matte skin against moist biological openings. Make the organ hierarchy
immediately readable: body first, feeding mouth second, dorsal root system
third. Keep value groups simple enough to survive conversion into 2D
beat-'em-up sprites.

## Sheet composition

A high-quality game production sheet on a restrained dark neutral workshop
background: one large full-body three-quarter hero view facing screen-left in a
low, predatory but unarmed stance; smaller clean front, exact side, and back
turnarounds; one close-up of the eyeless head and feeding mouth; one close-up of
the dorsal root organ. Keep every view consistent with the same individual and
anatomy. No text, labels, logos, borders, or watermark.

## Style

Semi-realistic painterly game concept art with sculptural 3D awareness,
controlled edge detail, readable silhouette, grounded materials, restrained
horror, and practical production clarity. Unusual and biological, not ornate.
This is a disposable first enemy whose body was grown for labor, not a boss
monster.
